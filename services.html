How Booking Works

From enquiry to event day.

Share Your Date

Tell us the date, city, venue and guest count through a call, WhatsApp or the booking form.

Walkthrough & Quote

We visit the venue (or review photos), suggest a layout, and send an itemised quote within 48 hours.

Confirm & Customise

Lock the date with a 25% advance and fine-tune colours, seating and menu-service details.

Setup & Celebrate

Our crew sets up a day ahead where possible, inspects everything, and stays on call through your event.

Common Questions

Before you book.

For weekend weddings in season (Nov–Feb), we recommend booking 3–4 months ahead. Birthdays, baby showers and housewarmings can usually be booked 2–3 weeks out, subject to availability.

We confirm a date with a 25% advance against your final quote. The balance is split into a pre-event instalment and a final settlement on the day, as agreed in your contract.

We are based in Thirumullaivoyal and regularly serve Ambattur, Avadi, Poonamallee, Red Hills and surrounding areas across Chennai. Longer-distance events elsewhere in Tamil Nadu can be arranged with advance notice.
