Our Story

Built by one family, run by fifty people who treat every event like their own.

Kamatenu Suppliers began in 2001 when founder P. Ponsrinivasan started renting shamiana tents for neighbourhood weddings out of a single godown. Word spread through Chennai's wedding halls and school grounds alike, and over the years the business added decor, balloon styling, photography, catering equipment and full event coordination to keep pace with requests from repeat clients.

Today, a second generation runs day-to-day operations alongside a 50-member crew of riggers, decorators, electricians, photographers and coordinators — all on staff, so the same standards apply whether it's a child's first birthday or an 800-guest wedding reception.

Our Journey

Milestones since 2001.

A quick look at how a two-tent rental stall became a full-service event supply house.

2001
Founded

Two tents, one godown, one neighbourhood

P. Ponsrinivasan begins renting shamiana tents for weddings in Thirumullaivoyal, working out of a single storage godown with a borrowed delivery van.

2006
Expansion

Decor, furniture and catering equipment added

Repeat clients start asking for more than tents. The catalogue grows to include mandap decor, chairs, tables and buffet equipment.

2011
Own Warehouse

A dedicated warehouse and a growing crew

Kamatenu Suppliers moves into its own warehouse on Chozhembedu Road and builds a permanent in-house crew of 20.

2015
New Services

Photography, balloon art and full coordination

In-house photography and videography, balloon styling, and a dedicated event-coordination service are introduced, alongside coverage in Ambattur and Avadi.

2019
1,000 Events

A thousand celebrations and counting

Kamatenu crosses 1,000 events supplied, and adds LED walls and professional sound engineering to the equipment list.

2023
Second Generation

New leadership, bigger crew

The founder's children step into day-to-day leadership. The in-house crew grows to 50 members across decor, electrical, catering and coordination.

2026
Today

2,200+ events across Chennai and Tamil Nadu

From weddings to school annual days, Kamatenu Suppliers now serves Chennai and the surrounding districts of Tamil Nadu with the same family-run standard it started with in 2001.
